01 — The project

Life Outside Your Bubble

The dizzying 24-hour news cycle leaves little room for reflection, context, and understanding — and we miss key points of view, often because they were never included. iPondr raises up new voices, explores overlooked topics, and reveals essential insights with depth and compassion, to spark genuine, caring conversations.

It's a digital media company trying to reinvent the role media plays in people's lives — high-quality journalism you can watch, hear, read, and ponder. Its mission is to bring you Life Outside Your Bubble: stories that create empathy and understanding rather than reactions.

What makes iPondr different

  • Every episode is anchored by a single fact that ties its stories together.
  • An episodic story model — closer to Netflix dropping a show than one-off articles.
  • Slow media: introspective, updated less often — not the daily news.
  • Radically transparent and fact-based, giving readers an equal, respectful voice.
  • Progressively inclusive — elevating overlooked stories from across “Authentic America,” with inclusivity in the content, the team, and the vendors.
  • Stories tied to the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals.

03 — The approach

From an idea to a launched platform

Phase 1

Discovery

Information architecture, taxonomy, sitemap, and look and feel for iPondr's new digital platform.

Phase 2

MLP — launched Nov 13, 2020

Defined the visual language and styleguide, produced the UX wireframes, and partnered with Unicorn on development — building a new way to consume the news that tells one story through many facets: audio, video, photo gallery, and writing.

“We had nothing but an idea — and a lot of trust.”

04 — The challenge

Building an end-to-end product from scratch

The client leaned on us for everything — subscription strategy, content, design language, and experience pillars. And they weren't afraid to go with wilder options; in fact they encouraged pushing the boundaries.

We had no requirements, no content, no copy, no subscription info — just a great team and a lot of trust. So we essentially created a completely new, end-to-end website from scratch.

05 — Design

The moments that made it iPondr

Homepage transition

Stories inside episodes

The homepage features an episode and the stories inside it, and lets you shuffle through to a different episode — introducing iPondr's unique content structure right away: stories nested inside episodes, moving readers away from the 24-hour cycle toward slower, thematically-linked storytelling.

Custom reactions

Stickers that encourage thoughtfulness

We created our own set of stickers to encourage thoughtful reactions at the end of an article — reactions that are considered, not reflexive.

Comments interaction

An equal, respectful voice

Comments are designed to give every reader a valid voice, regardless of opinion. Click on a stack of comments to reveal the rest — structured to be respectful, but heard.

Visual language

A system of shapes and colors

We built a visual identity for the different categories that stayed flexible — working just as well with images as without them.

Concept — iPSI

A data-exchange subscription

During the process we concepted iPSI: a subscription model that exchanges data instead of only money. Readers answer questions and share opinions to unlock articles — giving brands genuine public sentiment on the topics that matter to their audiences, and empowering their CSR.

06 — The goal

Empathy, for all of America

This was for all of America — reaching people across races, socioeconomic backgrounds, and life stages, and creating empathy between them. We wanted to foster curiosity about Life Outside Your Bubble, and to leave room for thoughtfulness and reflection after each article, so the reading meant something.
