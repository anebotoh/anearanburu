I’m Ane — a Basque digital product designer based in Brooklyn.

For the last 15 years, I’ve been designing and caring about pixels and the team that I work with.

I currently work at Output, designing and building music production tools. As part of a small team, I helped design and build Output Creator from 0 to 1 — a multi-year product redesign spanning workflow architecture, interaction design, visual design, micro-interactions, user interviews, opening my first PRs, and demoing the product at NAMM & SXSW in a partnership with Yamaha.

I’m interested in contributing to projects that have multi-layered complexity, are human-focused, and use technology responsibly.
